Hi, I'm Jasper, the developer behind ReframeKit. It's a free app I build in my spare time, and I'd love to hear from you.

## Get in touch

The fastest way to reach me is by email:

**[support@jasperwang.dev](mailto:support@jasperwang.dev)**

Whether it's a bug report, a feature request, or just feedback on how the app is working for you, send it over. I read every message.

## Reporting bugs

When reporting a bug, it helps if you can include:

- What you were doing when it happened
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Your device model and iOS version (optional but helpful)

## Feature requests

ReframeKit is shaped by the people who use it. If there's something missing that would make the app more useful to you, please let me know.

## Response time

I'm a solo developer, so replies usually come within a few days. Thanks for your patience.
